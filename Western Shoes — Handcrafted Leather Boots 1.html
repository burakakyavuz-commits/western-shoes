About Us

Western Shoes began with a simple belief: a good boot should outlast the road, the ranch, and the ride. Inspired by the open ranges and dusty highways of the American West, we set out to make footwear the way it used to be made, slowly, by hand, and from honest materials.

Every pair starts with 100% genuine full-grain cowhide, chosen for its strength and character. Our artisans hand-stitch each welt, shape wooden heels, and fit durable rubber outsoles built for gravel, pavement and pasture alike.

Our boots are for riders, ranchers, road-trippers and anyone who values a well-made thing. Wear them hard. They only get better.

Questions & Contact

What sizes do you offer?

US Men's 7-13 and US Women's 5-11, in whole and half sizes.

How do I care for my boots?

Brush off dust, condition the leather every few months, and let wet boots dry naturally away from heat.

Do you ship to my country?

Yes. We ship to the USA, Australia, the UK and Germany, with 30-day returns on unworn pairs.
